## Choosing a lab

Each lab lists its prerequisites on its first task page. If you do not have the
administrator role or the licenses a lab requires, work through it in a
[Webex developer sandbox organization](https://developer.webex.com/docs/developer-sandbox-guide)
rather than in a production tenant.
